Miners

One miner per line: its IP address or name, then an optional label. Example:

192.168.1.50 Garage
crsminer-rig1.local Office

IP addresses are the most reliable (phones often cannot resolve .local names). Give each miner a fixed IP in your router.

Find miners on my network

A browser cannot see .local names on its own, so the scan asks each address in the range once, on port 80. Enter your network prefix (the first three numbers of your router address). Several prefixes can be separated by commas.
